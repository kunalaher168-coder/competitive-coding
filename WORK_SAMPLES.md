**Arrays and optimization.** Worked on two-pointer, sliding-window, and prefix-aggregate cases. The public window example focuses on maintaining a valid invariant as the range expands and contracts.

**Simulation and dependency rules.** Worked on deterministic simulation and ordering problems, including conflict cases. The public scheduler and dependency planner show event ordering, tie handling, and cycle reporting.

**Interactive reasoning.** Worked on query strategies and bitmask-style state representations. No private interaction protocols or judge materials are included.
